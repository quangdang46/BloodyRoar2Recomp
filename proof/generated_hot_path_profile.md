## What this proves

1. **The generated hot path is ~90% CD/IRQ deferred-coroutine machinery**
   (`cd_intc_dispatch` + the `cd_*` §4 delegates) plus **one unnamed GTE
   renderer** (`0x8018866C`). Game-logic (`0x8012`–`0x8017`) is already
   cps_-covered and does NOT appear — clean coverage there is effective.

2. **`0x8018866C` is the single best non-CD port target**: unnamed,
   hot (924×/sample ≈ every sprite/poly emit), self-contained leaf
   (232 bytes, 7 blocks, no frame, args a0–a3). Pure GTE vertex/color
   attribute emission — loops `a3` times over a 100-byte-stride struct,
   `lwc2`/`mtc2`/`mfc2` + `gte_execute` ops `0x06`/`0x2E`/`0x1C`, stores
   transformed coords + packed color/attr, conditional second phase on a
   GTE clip flag. Safe to hand-port (no IRQ/MMIO boundary) BUT complex —
   inconsistent COP2 access (`gte_write_data` vs raw `gte_data[]` vs
   `gte_read_data`) and 3 distinct GTE ops → high mistranslation risk,
   hard to verify headless (rendering correctness).

3. **CD/IRQ functions are irreducibly §4** — they run generated by design
   (deferred-coroutine resume needs per-block `psx_check_interrupts_at` +
   live guest PC). The proper fix is per-instruction `psx_cyc_*` charging
   inside `cps_` bodies (`gen_cps_clean.py`), not hand ports.

## Recommended next port order

1. `0x8018866C` GTE emitter — highest-value tractable target; transcribe
   the exact `gte_*`/`lwc2`/`mfc2` sequence faithfully, verify frames
   advance + no GTE stall regression.
2. Unnamed CD fns `0x8019AF7C`/`0x8019B7E0` — large, §4-boundary, defer.
3. `0x8012`–`0x8017` cps_→readable-C sweep — volume, low risk, lower
   hot-path impact (already dispatch-clean).
